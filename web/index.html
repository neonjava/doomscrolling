Set up in a minute

1. Download and install the APK

Open the download on your Android 11+ phone. If asked, allow your browser to install this app. You can turn that permission off afterward.

2. Enable accessibility access

Open Doomscrolling, tap Accessibility access, and enable its service in Android Settings. This lets the app recognize video viewers and intercept swipes. It does not save screen content.

If Android shows “Restricted setting”, go to the app’s App info menu, select Allow restricted settings, then return to Accessibility.

3. Choose your apps and appearance

Turn on YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels protection. Pick System, Light, or Dark. Open a video and try swiping.

Early release: tested on one Android 16 phone. YouTube and Instagram layout changes may affect detection. See compatibility and known limits.
