THE PROJECT

Open source.
On your side.

Doomscrolling is a small Android app with one job: let you watch the short video you chose, then block swiping into the next. The source is public so you can understand it, change it, and help make it better.

Explore on GitHub ↗Contribute ↗

A Rust engine. A native Android app.

Rust handles viewer detection, gesture decisions, and warning timing. A small Kotlin layer connects to Android’s accessibility APIs and draws the interface. The website is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No backend is needed.

Help make the boundary better.

  • Report a detection or playback issue with your Android and app versions.
  • Understand the architecture before changing interception behavior.
  • Check the actual verification record, including known limitations.
  • Build it locally and contribute a focused change.

Early, and honest about it.

This is an alpha release. Third-party apps change their layouts, and accessibility services have platform limits. Compatibility is documented for tested versions; unsupported layouts are left usable instead of blocking an entire app.
