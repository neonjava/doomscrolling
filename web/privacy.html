PRIVACY NOTES

Your screen stays
on your phone.

Doomscrolling does not require an account, operate a backend, or collect your viewing history.

What the Android app reads

With accessibility access enabled, the service checks the foreground app and inspects the screen structure of supported YouTube and Instagram viewers. It uses element identifiers and bounds to decide where to intercept gestures. Accessibility actions preserve supported taps.

UI snapshots are processed in memory. Screen content, captions, messages, and viewing history are not written to app storage or sent to a server. The Android app does not request Internet permission.

What is saved

Only your protection switches and appearance preference are saved on your device. Operational diagnostics include the supported app package, protected bounds, and session counters; they do not include video content.

Your controls

Pause protection in the app, turn off individual supported apps, or revoke accessibility access in Android Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences.

This website and downloads

This website has no analytics or advertising scripts. It stores your appearance preference in your browser’s local storage. Hosting providers may process normal request information such as your IP address. Downloads and source links go to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

Questions or issues

Open a GitHub issue without personal screenshots, passwords, or private messages. For vulnerability reporting, follow the security policy.
